AM Radio Coverage Analysis

Interactive visualization and analysis of AM radio station coverage contours across the United States.

This project analyzes over 11,880 contour records from the FCC database, covering daytime 0.5 m/V and nighttime 2.0 m/V coverage patterns for thousands of AM stations. The analysis includes geographic regional breakdowns, power-based filtering, and on-demand contour visualization.

About the Data

The analysis is based on FCC contour data and station information from the AM broadcast database. Coverage areas are calculated using the Shoelace formula for polygon area computation. Geographic regions are assigned based on state boundaries and cultural/geographic similarities rather than strict time zones.
