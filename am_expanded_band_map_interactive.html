The AM expanded band — stations above 1600 on the dial

Every licensee between 1610 and 1700 kHz — the channels the FCC voted to begin populating on April 12, 1990 to relieve congestion on the standard AM dial. The U.S. uses the nine channels from 1620 through 1700; 1610 is reserved domestically for Travelers Information Stations but is in regular broadcast use in Canada and Mexico. America's first regular broadcaster on the expanded band was WJDM, which signed on at 1660 kHz in Elizabeth, N.J. on December 8, 1995. Today the band includes 49 U.S. stations, 7 Canadian stations and 10 Mexican licenses (3 Mexican sites appear on the map; the rest aren't geocoded in the RadioLand database).
